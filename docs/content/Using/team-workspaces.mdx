# Team Workspaces

Every team has a workspace: a place for the team's agents, knowledge, tools and prompts. The team owns what is in it, and everyone in the team can use it, according to their team role. **Personal** is your own workspace. It holds what you own, plus what people share with you.

Each item lives in exactly one workspace. Where it lives decides who owns it and who can reach it.

To create a team and add members, see [Teams and sharing](/Using/teams-and-sharing). For the team API and the audit events, see [Access Control & Teams](/Deploying/Access-Control#teams).

## Personal and team workspaces

| | Personal | A team workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who owns an item | You | The team. You stay listed as the person who added it |
| Who can reach it | You, plus the people and teams you share it with, item by item | Everyone in the team, by their team role |
| Folders | Your own | The team's, the same for everyone in it |
| What its agents can use | Your items, and items shared with you | The team's items only |
| When someone leaves the team | Their items leave with them | Team items stay with the team |

A team agent uses only the team's knowledge, tools and prompts. Nobody's personal credentials sit behind a team agent, so it works the same for every member.

## Switch workspace

The name at the top of the sidebar is the workspace switcher. It lists **Personal** ("Only you"), then your **Teams**, each with its member count and your role, then **Manage teams** and **Create team**.

The workspace you pick is the scope of the app:

- the **Agents**, **Knowledge**, **Tools** and **Prompts** pages, and their folders,
- the chats in the sidebar, and chat search,
- [Activity](/Using/activity) and your pinned agents,
- the pickers in the chat composer and the agent builder.

Under its intro, each of these pages names the workspace and who can reach it, for example "Operations · Everyone in the team can use what's here · 12 people", or "Personal · Only you, unless you share an item".

When you switch, a list page stays open and shows the other workspace's items. An item page, such as an agent or a chat, goes back to its list. **General** and **Access tokens** in Settings are yours, whatever the workspace.

DocsGPT remembers your workspace in this browser. Each browser tab keeps its own: switching in one tab doesn't change the others.

A link to a team item or a team chat switches to its workspace, and DocsGPT says so ("Switched to Operations"). If you're no longer in the team, DocsGPT switches to Personal and tells you.

In a team workspace, the sidebar has a **Team** entry that opens the team's page: its **Members**, **Personal items shared**, and, for Admins, [**Usage**](#usage) and **Settings**.

## Team roles

Your role is the same on everything in the workspace. A team item's tile names who added it rather than your access.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Chat with the team's agents and pin them. Use the team's knowledge, tools and prompts, browse its sources, and duplicate its prompts. |
| **Editor** | Everything a Viewer can, plus create, import, edit and export items, see an agent's logs and manage its schedules (and [take one over](#take-over)), create folders and file items in them, and move their own items in from Personal. A new agent from an Editor is a draft until an Admin publishes it. |
| **Admin** | Everything an Editor can, plus publish agents and manage outside access (API key and widget, public link, webhook) and take it over, delete items, move items out to their own Personal, add and remove members and change their roles, see the team's [Usage](#usage), [export its chats](#export-the-teams-chats) when the instance allows it, and edit the team's name and description. |
| **Owner** | The person who created the team, or was given it. An Admin who can also transfer ownership and delete the team. |

New members join as Viewers. A team always keeps at least one Admin, and the Owner can't be demoted or removed: to step down, transfer ownership first, under the team's **Settings**.

<Callout type="info">
  After upgrading to this release, a team's former members are Viewers and its admins are Admins. Give Editor to the people who should create and change the team's items.
</Callout>

## What each page shows

The **Agents**, **Knowledge**, **Tools** and **Prompts** pages follow the same order:

1. **Folders**: the workspace's folders that hold this kind of item, plus empty folders.
2. The items in no folder.
3. In Personal only, **Shared with you**: items other people shared with you, each with its owner and your access, **Viewer** or **Editor**.

In a team, **New**, **Add**, **Import** and **New folder** appear only for Editors and up. On the Agents page, templates are there in both workspaces: an Editor's copy of a template lands in the team as a draft. **Discovered** is in Personal only.

**Connectors** and **Custom models** are personal. In a team, those pages say so and offer **Switch to Personal**. A team can't hold a source synced from a connected service, or a tool that signs in with an account, so the tool forms leave those out in a team.

## Folders

A workspace has one folder tree, and a folder can hold agents, knowledge, tools and prompts together. Each page shows its own part of the folder. A tile reads, for example, "6 prompts · also 2 agents, 3 tools", and an open folder has **Also in this folder**, with links to the same folder on the other pages.

- Folders nest up to three levels deep.
- Team folders are the same for everyone in the team. Editors and up create, rename, move and delete folders and file items in them; Viewers browse them.
- Deleting a folder never deletes what it holds: its items and folders move up to its parent.
- In Personal, you can file your own items and items shared with you. A team's items can't go in a personal folder; pin a team agent instead.
- When you pick tools or knowledge for an agent or a workflow that sits in a folder, the picker lists that folder's items first ("In Support, this agent's folder").
- A Knowledge folder on a team agent is the team's folder, so every member sees the same folder and the same sources in it.

For the folder pages themselves, see [Organize knowledge in folders](/Sources/adding-knowledge#organize-knowledge-in-folders) and [Organize agents in folders](/Using/web-app#organize-agents-in-folders).

## Move to team and back

Moving is how existing work gets into a team. Choose **Move to team…** in the **⋯** menu of an agent, a source, a tool, a prompt or a folder. In a team, an Admin's menu has **Move to Personal…** to take an item out. A move changes the owner in place: ids, API keys, links and history stay the same.

| Move | Who can |
| --- | --- |
| Into a team | The item's owner, if they are an Editor or above in that team |
| Into a team, an agent with outside access (API key, public link or webhook) | The item's owner, if they are an Admin in that team. An Editor turns its outside access off first, or asks an Admin |
| Out of a team, into your own Personal | An Admin of the team |

### The plan

Before anything moves, the dialog shows the whole plan. Pick the team and, if you like, a folder there; then check:

- **Who gains or loses access.** Everyone in the team gets access by their role. People you shared the item with outside the team lose access, by name, because a team item has no per-item shares.
- **Who owns it.** "Operations will own it. It stays with the team if you leave. You stay listed as the person who added it."
- **Moves along.** What the item brings with it, and why: used by the agent, in the folder, or on the same workflow.
- **Can't move.** What stays behind, and why.
- **Renamed to avoid a clash.** A folder or an agent's address that already exists in the team gets a new name.
- **Nothing changes about who pays.** Schedules and outside access keep their payer.
- **Chats.** How many chats with a moving agent move along.

If something changed while the dialog was open, the plan reloads with "Something changed; check again". Choose **Move to Operations** to apply it, all at once or not at all.

### What moves along

- **An agent** brings what it uses that you own: its sources, tools and prompt, the Knowledge folders it reads, and its workflow, with every other agent of yours on that workflow. From a Knowledge folder, only the sources and sub-folders come; agents, tools and prompts filed in it stay in Personal, unfiled.
- **A folder** brings its sub-folders and everything you filed in it.
- **Chats** with a moving agent follow it into the team, so its members keep their history.
- Per-item shares and access settings on a moved item are removed, and saved secrets are sealed again for the team.

### What can't move

A team item can't depend on one person's account, device or permission, so these stay personal:

| Item | Why | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tool signed in with an account (a connected service, or an MCP server you sign in to) | The connection is personal | Taken off the moving agent |
| A source synced from a connected service | The sync runs on your connection | Taken off the moving agent |
| A personal custom model | Custom models are personal | Taken off the moving agent |
| A tool that runs on a paired device | It runs on your machine | Taken off the moving agent |
| Someone else's item, such as a source shared with you | It isn't yours to move | Taken off the moving agent; it stays with its owner |
| A tool whose saved secret can't be read any more | It can't be sealed for the team | Enter the secret again, then move |

### When a move is refused

- **An agent people outside the team can reach would use what moves.** An API key, public link or webhook must not serve a team's item. Move that agent too, turn its outside access off, or take the items off it first. A personal agent of yours without outside access can keep using what moved, but can't be published while it does.
- **Moving out, a team agent that stays still uses what moves.** A team agent can't use a personal item. Move that agent too, or take the items off it first.

Moving out, only your own chats with the agent go back to Personal. Other members can still read their old chats with it, but can't continue them.

## Sharing and where access comes from

Menus say **Share**. What the dialog shows depends on where the item lives.

**A team item** has no per-item people: access follows team membership. The dialog says "It belongs to Operations. Access follows team membership", with one row for everyone in the team and its member count, and your own role ("Admin in Operations · From the team"). Admins get **Manage members**. For an agent, **Outside the team** lists its public link, API key and widget, and webhook, each **Off**, or **On** with who pays for it. Only Admins change those, on the agent's own page.

**A personal item** keeps per-item sharing with teams and people (see [Teams and sharing](/Using/teams-and-sharing#share-with-a-team-or-a-teammate)). Each row says where the access comes from: **Added by you**, **via Operations**, or **Shared, not owned by the team**. For each team you're an Editor or above in, the dialog asks **Should Operations own this?** and offers **Move to team…**.

A team's page lists, under **Personal items shared**, the personal items members share with it. They belong to the people who shared them, not to the team, so they would leave with them. Ask the owner to move the ones the team depends on.

## When someone leaves a team

- **Team items stay.** Items they added belong to the team and keep working. They lose access to them. If they're added again, they get it back.
- **Their chats in the team** can no longer be opened by them, and nobody else can open them either. If they're added again, the chats come back.
- **Their personal items stay theirs.** What they shared with the team pauses: nobody in the team reaches it while they're out. If they're added again, those shares work again. Before removing someone, the dialog lists the personal items they share with the team, so you can ask them to move the ones the team depends on first.
- **What they paid for on team agents pauses.** The schedules they set pause, and so does outside access they pay for. Nothing is deleted: it runs again if they're added back with the role, or once someone [takes it over](#take-over). See [What pauses, and when it resumes](#what-pauses-and-when-it-resumes).

The same pause follows a role change: schedules a member set pause when they drop to Viewer, and outside access they pay for pauses when they drop below Admin. When a removal or a demotion pauses anything, the team's Owner and Admins get one notification that names it, for example "Dana left Operations: 2 schedules and outside access on 1 agent are paused until someone takes over." Whoever made the change isn't notified, and the person who left isn't either. Its link opens the team's [Usage](#usage) tab, which lists everything paused.

A team that still owns items can't be deleted. Move its items to Personal or delete them first; the team's **Settings** names what is left.

## What stays personal

- **Connections and connectors.** Accounts you connect, and the tools and synced sources that use them, stay in Personal.
- **Custom models.** A team agent uses the models your instance offers, not a member's custom model.
- **Paired devices.** A remote device runs on one person's machine.
- **Access tokens.** A token acts as you; see [Over the API](#over-the-api).
- **Your chats.** A chat belongs to the workspace you started it in and can use only that workspace's items. In a team, the composer says "Operations · Only you can see this chat". Other members, Admins included, can't open your chats. If the instance allows it, the team's Owner and Admins can [export the team's chats](#export-the-teams-chats) to a file, and a new chat in that team says so. As with any agent, a team agent's **Logs** and **Insights**, which Editors and up see, record each run, the question included.

A team chat can be shared only as a read-only link, and only by an Admin. **Delete all chats** in **Settings → General** deletes your chats in the current workspace only.

## Who pays

Whoever starts a run pays for it, from their own allowance: the [usage quota](/Deploying/Usage-Quotas) an instance admin sets for each person. A team has no budget of its own, and where an agent lives or who owns it never decides who pays. Moving an agent doesn't change it either.

| Run | Who pays |
| --- | --- |
| A chat, with or without an agent, in Personal or in a team | The person chatting |
| A schedule's runs | The person who set the schedule |
| An agent's API key and widget, public link, webhook, `/v1` and MCP traffic | On a team agent, the Admin who turned its outside access on, or who took it over. On a personal agent, its owner |
| A background continuation, a retry, a chat's title, a workflow step | Whoever pays for the run it belongs to |

An agent's own limit (tokens or requests per 24 hours) applies on top, whoever pays. One run has one payer, fixed when it starts, and the limits are checked again before each of its model calls; see [Usage Quotas](/Deploying/Usage-Quotas#windows-and-enforcement).

A chat never names its payer, because it is always the person chatting. A schedule says who pays for it: "Paid by Dana", or "Paid by you". On a team agent, the share dialog and the agent's Access Details name who pays for its outside access.

### What pauses, and when it resumes

A schedule, or a team agent's outside access, pauses when its payer can't pay. Nothing is deleted and nothing needs to be switched back on: it runs again by itself once the reason clears. The item shows why it is paused, for example "Dana is out of allowance until 1 Dec."

| Reason | Clears when |
| --- | --- |
| The payer is out of allowance | The allowance resets, an instance admin raises it, or someone takes it over |
| The payer left the team, or lost the role it needs: Editor for a schedule, Admin for outside access | They're added back with that role, or someone takes it over |
| Nobody pays for it | Someone takes it over |
| The account it runs on is deactivated | The account is reactivated. On a team agent, someone can also take it over |

While outside access is paused, the agent's API key, widget, public link, webhook, `/v1` and MCP callers are told only "This agent is paused, so it can't answer right now.", never whose account it is or why. Members still chat with the agent in the app, each on their own allowance. A paused schedule skips its runs; see [Paid by and paused schedules](/Agents/schedules#paid-by-and-paused-schedules).

A team source's scheduled sync runs for the person who added the source while they are an Editor or above in the team. Otherwise its scheduled syncs are skipped and the source shows its sync as paused. A paused sync can't be taken over; an Editor can still sync the source by hand.

### Take over

**Take over** makes you the payer of a schedule or of an agent's outside access, by your own click. Nobody becomes a payer any other way.

| What | Who can take it over |
| --- | --- |
| A schedule on a team agent | Editors and up |
| A team agent's outside access | Admins and the Owner |
| Anything on a personal agent | Nobody: its owner pays for its outside access, and a schedule stays with the person it is stored under |

Take over is offered on any running or paused schedule and on any agent with outside access on, not only on paused ones, unless you already pay for it. It asks you to confirm once:

- You pay for it from then on. What it used so far stays with the earlier payer, and a run already going finishes on their allowance.
- The agent's own limit still applies.
- A schedule you take over starts its failure count again, and is no longer linked to the chat it was set from: that chat stays private to the person who had it.

Take over isn't available with a [personal access token](/API/personal-access-tokens); sign in to the app. Each one is recorded in the [audit log](/Deploying/Access-Control#audit-log) as `team.schedule_taken_over` or `team.outside_access_taken_over`, with who paid before and who pays now.

## Usage

The team's Owner and Admins have a **Usage** tab on the team's page. It reports what was used in the team workspace:

- **This period or the previous one.** A period is the instance's quota window ([`QUOTA_PERIOD`](/Deploying/Settings-Reference#quotas): a day, a week or a month, in UTC).
- **Chats and runs without a person.** Chats count what members ran themselves, plain chats without an agent included. Runs without a person are schedules, an agent's outside traffic and other background work.
- **By person**, with each person's role. People who have left the team stay listed with what they used.
- **By agent**, with its requests, how many people used it, whether its own limit is on, and who pays for its outside access.
- **What is paused**: every schedule, outside access and scheduled source sync in the team that is paused for its payer, with the reason and who paid. Each schedule or outside access links to the agent's page, where the right role can [take it over](#take-over).

Amounts are in dollars where the models have a price, and in tokens otherwise. Only use in this workspace counts: a member's Personal use never shows here, and an agent's use stays in the report after it is moved out. Use recorded before this release isn't in it.

The Usage tab is a report, not a limit. Nothing in it can be capped or edited: each person's allowance stays their own, set by an instance admin under [Usage Quotas](/Deploying/Usage-Quotas).

## Export the team's chats

Team chats are private to the person who started them. An operator can let a team's Owner and Admins export them all, by setting `TEAM_CHAT_EXPORT_ENABLED=true` (off by default). There is no screen for browsing other people's chats: only one file.

With the setting on:

- The team's **Settings** tab has an export row for its Owner and Admins. It downloads one NDJSON file, `docsgpt-<team>-chats-<date>.ndjson`, with every chat started in that team workspace, including chats of people who have since left and chats through the team's agents from outside (API key, widget, public link).
- Each chat keeps its name, who started it, its agent, and each message's prompt and answer, time, model, source titles, tool names, attachment file names and feedback.
- The file never holds personal chats, other teams' chats, tool arguments or results, reasoning, or attachment contents.
- Members see "Admins of Operations can export chats here" when they start a new chat in the team.
- Each export is recorded in the [audit log](/Deploying/Access-Control#audit-log) as `team.chats_exported`, with how many chats and people it covered.

## Over the API

Requests work in Personal unless they name a team. Send the `X-DocsGPT-Workspace` header with a team id, or `personal`. Where a client can't set headers, send the `workspace` query parameter instead. Only lists, creates and new chats read it; a request about one item goes by that item's own workspace. `GET /api/workspaces` lists your workspaces and your role in each. See [Work in a team workspace](/API#work-in-a-team-workspace).
