# Teams and Sharing

A team is a group of people on the same DocsGPT instance who work on agents, sources, prompts and tools together. Any signed-in user can create a team. A team gets two things:

- **A team workspace**, which owns the agents, knowledge, tools and prompts made in it or moved into it. Everyone in the team reaches them by their team role. See [Team workspaces](/Using/team-workspaces).
- **Sharing** for your personal items: you stay the owner, and the teams or people you share with get **Viewer** or **Editor** access to that one item.

Teams are useful on an instance where each person signs in with their own account, which in practice means [SSO with OIDC](/Deploying/OIDC-SSO). This page covers creating a team, its members and sharing personal items. For the roles model, the team API and exactly what runs with whose access, see [Access Control & Teams](/Deploying/Access-Control#teams).

## Create a team

1. Open **Settings → Teams**, or open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and choose **Create team**.
2. Choose **New team**, enter a **Team name**, then choose **Create**. To add a description, open the team and choose **Edit**.

You are the team's owner and one of its admins.

## Add members

1. In **Settings → Teams**, open the team. On its **Members** tab, choose **Add member**.
2. Enter the person's email under **Member email**, pick a **Role** (**Viewer**, the default, **Editor** or **Admin**), and choose **Add**.

The person must have signed in to DocsGPT at least once. Otherwise DocsGPT can't find them and shows "No user with that email — they must sign in once first." New members get an **Added to a team** notification.

## Roles

A team has four roles: **Viewer**, **Editor**, **Admin** and **Owner**. They decide what each member can do in the team's workspace; see [Team roles](/Using/team-workspaces#team-roles). Admins can also remove anything shared with the team.

A team's page has three tabs:

- **Members**: Admins change a member's role from the drop-down next to their name and remove them with **Remove**. The removal dialog says that the team items they added stay with the team, and lists the personal items they share with it.
- **Personal items shared**: the personal items members share with the team. They belong to the people who shared them, not to the team.
- **Settings** (Admins): the team's name and description, **Transfer ownership** (the Owner only) and **Delete team** (the Owner, once the team owns nothing).

A team always keeps at least one Admin, and the Owner can't be demoted or removed. The web app has no button to leave a team; use the [team API](/Deploying/Access-Control#managing-a-team) for that.

## Switch workspace

The name at the top of the sidebar is the workspace switcher. It lists **Personal** and your teams, with **Manage teams** and **Create team**. Switching changes what the app shows: the Agents, Knowledge, Tools and Prompts pages, chats, Activity and pins all follow the workspace. See [Switch workspace](/Using/team-workspaces#switch-workspace).

## Share with a team or a teammate

Sharing is for your personal items. A team's own items need no sharing: everyone in the team reaches them by their role.

1. Open the menu of the item and choose **Share**:
   - an agent: on its card in **Agents**, or in the agent editor,
   - a source: in **Settings → Knowledge**,
   - a tool: in **Settings → Tools**,
   - a prompt: on its card in **Settings → Prompts**.
2. Under **Add people or teams…**, pick a whole team or one person from your teams.
3. Choose **Viewer** or **Editor**, then **Done**.

Each row in the dialog says where that access comes from: **Added by you**, **via** a team, or **Shared, not owned by the team**. For each team you're an Editor or above in, the dialog also asks whether the team should own the item and offers **Move to team…**; see [Move to team and back](/Using/team-workspaces#move-to-team-and-back).

| Item | Viewers | Editors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | Chat with it and pin it | Edit it, publish it, see logs and manage schedules |
| Source | Browse, search and use it in their agents | Edit chunks and files, sync, change settings |
| Tool | Use it inside the owner's agents and, by default, in their own agents | Change actions and approvals, and by default its credentials |
| Prompt | Read it, use it in their agents and, by default, duplicate it | Change the text |

As the owner, you can change this under **Access settings** in the same dialog. Some switches are off by default and widen access, such as **Editors can share**, **Editors can delete**, **Editors can manage access details** and **Viewers can see logs**; by default, only the owner shares, deletes, or creates an agent's API key, public link and webhook. Others are on by default and narrow access when you turn them off:

- sources: **Viewers can see settings**,
- tools: **Editors can change credentials and connection** and **Viewers can use it in their own agents**,
- prompts: **Viewers can duplicate**.

A tool or source someone shared with you works in your own agents and chats, but not beyond them: an agent that uses one can't have an API key, public link or webhook, and you can't add one to an agent that has any of them. Publishing such an agent makes it available in your own chats without creating an API key. See [Agents that use someone else's tools or sources](/Deploying/Access-Control#agents-that-use-someone-elses-tools-or-sources).

Sharing an agent doesn't share the tools, sources and prompt it uses; people reach them only through the agent, with the access the dialog lists under **What this agent uses**. Shared tools run with the owner's saved credentials, and nobody you share with can read them. See [Sharing agents and what they use](/Deploying/Access-Control#sharing-agents-and-what-they-use).

## Where shared items appear

Items shared with you are in your Personal workspace. The **Agents**, **Knowledge**, **Tools** and **Prompts** pages list them under **Shared with you**, after your own items, and the pickers in chats and the agent builder offer them too. The **Discovered** agents are different: they are public agents you opened from a link.

The cards of shared agents, sources, tools and prompts name the owner and your role, **Viewer** or **Editor**, in their last line. Each share also sends a **Shared with you** notification.

### Your folders

Your personal folders belong to you; they aren't shared with a team. You can file items teammates share with you in your own folders, with **Move to folder** on the item's menu, the same as your own. Filing an item doesn't change it, and nobody else sees where you filed it. A team's own items go in the team's folders, which are the same for everyone in it; see [Folders](/Using/team-workspaces#folders).

You can give an agent a whole Knowledge folder of yours, including an agent a teammate shared with you as an editor. Teammates who open the agent see the folder by name, with whose it is. Adding it to a teammate's agent asks you to confirm first, because sources you file in it later run with your access too. Its editors can remove it but can't change what's in it; only you choose what you file there. On a teammate's agent, your folder gives it only the sources the agent's owner can use and the sources you own or edit; once you can no longer edit the agent, the folder stops and gives the agent nothing. See [Pick a whole folder](/Sources/adding-knowledge#pick-a-whole-folder).

## Stop sharing

- To remove one grant, open **Share** on the item and choose **Remove access** next to the team or person.
- On a team's **Personal items shared** tab, open an item to see who has access in that team and choose **Remove access**, or choose **Manage sharing** to open its share dialog.

Only the owner, an editor allowed to share, or a team admin can remove a share.

<Callout type="info">
  Team shares are only for signed-in users on your instance. To let anyone read a conversation, see [Sharing conversations](/Using/sharing). To publish an agent to the public, use its **Access Details** (API key, public link or widget).
</Callout>
